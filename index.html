Privacy Policy

Flash publish by R&S telecom
Last updated: 15 September 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Chrome extension Flash publish by R&S telecom (“the Extension”) handles information when you use it to prepare and publish job offers from Atlassian to Apec, Turnover-IT, and LinkedIn.

1. Single purpose

The Extension helps authorized recruiters anonymize and publish job descriptions from Atlassian issues to selected job platforms. It does not provide unrelated browsing, advertising, or tracking features.

2. Data the Extension processes

  • Job / issue content you choose to process (titles, descriptions, and related fields from Atlassian or the publish flow).
  • API credentials you configure (OpenRouter API keys, optional EmailJS credentials if email verification is enabled, optional Turnover JobConnect token), stored locally in Chrome extension storage.
  • Publish session state needed to complete the workflow (selected platforms, draft text, process status).

Email one-time password (OTP) verification is currently disabled in the Extension. When it is disabled, the Extension does not collect email addresses for verification and does not send OTP emails.

The Extension does not sell personal data and does not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

Depending on features you use, data may be sent to:

  • OpenRouter — job/issue text for AI anonymization and extraction when you run those features (requires your API key).
  • EmailJS — only if email verification is re-enabled and you configure EmailJS credentials in the Extension settings. In that case, your email address and OTP content are sent to EmailJS to deliver verification emails. The Extension does not ship built-in EmailJS credentials; you must supply your own.
  • Apec, LinkedIn, Turnover-IT, and Atlassian — when you explicitly run publish or extraction flows on those sites while signed into your own accounts. Publish automation uses your existing browser sessions on those sites; it does not collect your board passwords.

Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies. The Extension does not execute remotely hosted JavaScript as extension logic; AI/API calls exchange data only.

4. Storage and retention

Credentials and session data are stored locally via Chrome storage on your device/profile. They remain until you clear them in the Extension settings, clear site/extension data in Chrome, or uninstall the Extension.

5. Limited use

Data is used only to provide the Extension’s flash-publish features (anonymize, extract, and publish). It is not used to build unrelated user profiles and is not sold to third parties.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices

  • Do not run publish/anonymize flows if you do not want content sent to OpenRouter or job boards.
  • Review LinkedIn submit and checkout options before confirming publish; checkout steps may involve LinkedIn fees.
  • Remove API keys from the Extension popup / settings when no longer needed.
  • Uninstall the Extension to stop all processing and remove local extension storage associated with it.

7. Contact

For privacy questions about this Extension, contact R&S telecom at contact@rs-telecom.fr.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the Extension’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly.
